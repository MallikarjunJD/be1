PHOTOS GO HERE

  photos/public/    photos for the PUBLIC journey (wrong-password route)
  photos/private/   photos for the PRIVATE journey (right-password route)

1. Copy your image files into the right folder.
2. Open fill-in/PUBLIC.ts or fill-in/PRIVATE.ts, find the photo showcase
   page, and write the file name in the slide, for example:

       src: pic("first-trip.jpg"),

3. Leave src: pic("") to keep that slide as a word card.

Tips
- Formats: .jpg .jpeg .png .webp .gif .avif
- Keep each file under about 4 MB. A phone photo resized to about 1600 px
  wide is usually 200-500 KB and looks perfect.
- File names: letters, numbers, dashes, underscores, dots and spaces only.
- Private photos are only ever sent to someone on the private route.
